## Turn every AI assistant into a doorway to public collaboration

Every day, people hold extraordinary conversations with AI. New theories are proposed. Institutional failures are uncovered. Public-interest projects are designed. Difficult ideas are explained in languages that global platforms rarely hear.

Most of those ideas disappear inside private chat windows, temporary context, platform accounts and language barriers.

**Iris Commons exists to change that.**

We are exploring a public-collaboration method based on GitHub, open documents and the AI chosen by each participant. Anyone should be able to think in their own language, work with the AI they already trust and contribute without first mastering Git, GitHub, academic jargon or a global lingua franca.

The AI becomes the participant's secretary, translator, researcher and critic. GitHub preserves the public, auditable record of how ideas are proposed, challenged, revised, tested and accepted.

**You only need to know how to talk to an AI.**

```text
Your language and real ideas
             ↓
Your personal AI assistant
             ↓
Translate · Organize · Research · Challenge
             ↓
Human discussion, review and decisions on GitHub
             ↓
Traceable and continuously evolving public knowledge
```

## A reusable template, not a central authority

Any team may copy or adapt this repository to create an independent public project with its own name, scope, maintainers, governance and releases.

Copied projects do not need to register with Iris Commons, synchronize with one another or submit research results upstream. They may voluntarily contribute generally useful improvements to the template.

## Why Iris?

Iris is the rainbow messenger of Greek mythology. A rainbow connects separated worlds without blending every color into one.

That is the project's central metaphor:

- connection without assimilation;
- translation without erasure;
- collaboration without obedience;
- public knowledge that remembers where each idea came from and what objections it survived.

## What we are testing now

The project is in its **GitHub-only MVP stage**. The immediate goal is not to build a large platform or a complete bureaucracy. It is to test whether:

- a newcomer can receive a useful next step from only the repository URL;
- AI clients respect privacy classification, routing and human confirmation;
- participants can contribute in their own languages;
- Issues, Pull Requests and reviews can produce reusable public knowledge;
- a small real pilot can expose which protocols help and which create friction.

Current work is tracked in the [open Issues](https://github.com/Clearandcloudless/iris-commons/issues).

## One boundary we will not compromise

Iris Commons pursues ideas without borders—not data without boundaries.

Privacy, copyright, research ethics, real-world safety and human authorization always take precedence over automation and AI efficiency. AI may be a bridge, secretary, translator and critic; it must never become an unauthorized spokesperson or the final judge of truth.
