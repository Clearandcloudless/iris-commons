# Research

`research/` contains structured, reusable research work derived from public discussion.

Planned sections:

```text
research/questions/   sustained research questions and hypotheses
research/evidence/    reusable evidence reviews and source records
research/pilots/      public-interest pilot projects
research/results/     reports, reproductions and validated or disputed results
```

A new idea should normally begin as an Issue. Create a research file only when the work needs a maintained method, evidence set, experiment, report or reproducibility record.

Research records should state:

- question and hypothesis;
- method;
- data sources and licenses;
- software, model and environment versions;
- results and uncertainty;
- limitations and counterexamples;
- reproduction or verification status;
- human and AI roles;
- originating Issues and Pull Requests.

Raw personal conversations, unreviewed uploads, restricted data and large binary datasets do not belong here. Public files must be classified `D0` under [`DATA_CLASSIFICATION.md`](../DATA_CLASSIFICATION.md).
