# Research Results

This directory contains maintained reports, reproductions, validation records and disputed results produced by Iris Commons research work.

A result should link to the original question or pilot and include:

- method and data provenance;
- software, model and environment versions;
- results and uncertainty;
- limitations, counterexamples and conflicting findings;
- reproduction or review status;
- human and AI roles;
- license and `D0 public-ready` confirmation.

A result may remain `disputed` or `inconclusive`. Do not rewrite uncertainty as consensus.
