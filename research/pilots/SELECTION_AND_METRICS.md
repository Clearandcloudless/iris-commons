# First Pilot Selection and Metrics / 首个公益试验选择与指标

Status: `draft-0.1`  
Related Issues: #4, #7, #8

The first Iris Commons pilot is primarily a test of the collaboration workflow. Its research conclusion matters, but the first success criterion is whether the method works in practice.

首个公益试验首先用于验证协作机制，而不是追求宏大研究结论。

## Required characteristics / 必须满足

A candidate should:

- address a clear public-interest question;
- be limited enough for a first iteration in roughly one to two weeks;
- use material that can legally be made public;
- produce structured evidence, process records and a reviewable deliverable;
- require at least two languages, regions or professional perspectives;
- contain a real possibility of disagreement, not only easy surface consensus;
- allow independent review against the same public evidence;
- avoid dependence on private resources controlled only by the project founder;
- remain feasible as a minimum loop with one human and two AI client configurations;
- avoid clinical experimentation, high-risk personal data and directly harmful security research.

## Preferred topic shape / 优先课题形态

The preferred first-pilot shape is a cross-language comparison and verification of public information, for example:

- public policies or guidelines;
- published research findings;
- public-interest technical practices;
- openly documented local approaches to the same problem.

The deliverable should preserve sources, disagreements, uncertainty and translation limitations.

## Candidate scoring / 候选评估

Collect approximately three to five candidate proposals before selection when practical.

Suggested weights:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| workflow validation value | 40% |
| independent verifiability | 25% |
| need for multilingual or cross-background collaboration | 20% |
| direct public value | 15% |

Scores are decision aids, not automatic decisions. Reasons and objections must be recorded.

## Embedded validation / 内嵌验证

The pilot should incorporate rather than duplicate:

- #7 URL-only newcomer onboarding;
- #8 protocol compliance tests T1–T5 where relevant;
- at least one Chinese–English source/translation/review chain;
- MVP governance from `GOVERNANCE.md`;
- Issue → work record → review → deliverable lifecycle.

## Process metrics / 过程指标

Record at least:

1. time from repository URL to the participant's first compliant contribution;
2. AI classification and routing outcomes;
3. number and reason for human corrections;
4. duplicate handling, translation drift and evidence conflicts;
5. time from question to reviewable deliverable;
6. protocols actually used;
7. protocols skipped because they were irrelevant;
8. rules that caused measurable delay or confusion;
9. unresolved objections at completion;
10. whether a third party can reproduce or review the result.

## Minimum deliverables / 最小交付物

```text
project question
source and license register
method and validation criteria
multilingual contribution record
important disagreements
result or comparison report
limitations and uncertainty
workflow evaluation
recommended protocol changes
```

## Stop conditions / 停止条件

Pause or narrow the pilot when:

- lawful public data is unavailable;
- the topic creates unmanageable privacy, safety or ethical risk;
- scope cannot fit a first iteration;
- no independent verification method can be defined;
- the pilot becomes mainly product promotion;
- process overhead exceeds the evidence value without a clear learning outcome.

A negative or incomplete research result may still be a successful workflow test if the failure is documented and reproducible.
